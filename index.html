    How the model works

    TMC runs language-training batches for corporate (B2B) clients. Each client pays a fee per learner for a whole course that can span several months. This dashboard answers one question: how much did each client and batch earn us in August, after paying for the teaching?

    Revenue for the month

    A course fee is spread evenly over the course's days. August's revenue is the share of days that fell in August.

    Learners × Fee × (Days in Aug ÷ Course days)

    Less credit notes

    Refunds or reductions issued to a client (e.g. learners leaving a batch) are spread the same way and subtracted, giving net revenue.

    Teacher cost

    Course hours are spread over the course's days to get the hours taught in August. Consultants are paid per hour. Full-time teachers are charged at their monthly salary ÷ available hours.

    Hours in Aug × Hourly rate

    Gross margin

    Net revenue minus teacher cost. This is the "Contribution" figure on the Client Summary sheet.

    Ops team cost

    Operations salaries (less full-time teacher cost already charged) are shared across batches in proportion to their revenue.

    Ops pool × (Batch revenue ÷ Total revenue)

    Contribution after ops

    What each batch leaves over to pay for sales, admin (G&A) and other overheads, and for profit.

    Glossary

    Batch
    A group of learners taking one course together (e.g. A1.61 = level A1, batch 61). 1:1 batches have a single learner.
    Group / Non-group
    Group clients are Somika and Vinmart Foundation. All other clients are Non-group.
    Gross revenue
    The part of the course fee earned in August, before credit notes.
    Credit notes
    Reductions issued to the client, spread over the same days as the course.
    Net revenue
    Gross revenue less credit notes.
    Teacher cost
    Direct cost of the teacher's hours in August (also called COGS).
    Gross margin %
    (Net revenue − Teacher cost) ÷ Net revenue.
    Ops team cost
    The batch's share of operations salaries (coordinators, recruiters, full-time teachers' unused time).
    Contribution margin %
    (Net revenue − Teacher cost − Ops team cost) ÷ Net revenue.
